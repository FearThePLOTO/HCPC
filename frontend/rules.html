Rules and About HCPC 2026

How to play. ICPC inspired, 8-bit edition. Operated by Huawei.

About HCPC
HCPC is a solo quest for Egyptian university students. Test your logic, algorithms, and data structures across two levels: Qualification (online) and Finals (onsite in Cairo). Earn XP, clear bosses, reach the leaderboard.

Who Can Play

  • Enrolled in an Egyptian university (1st to 4th year) or graduated within one year.
  • Solo run only. No co-op.
  • University email or student ID may be asked at check-in.

Game Format

  • Level 1 - Qualification: 5 hours, 8 to 10 puzzles, online on Huawei Cloud. Clear as many as you can.
  • Level 2 - Finals: 5 hours, onsite, proctored. Scoring like ICPC: most solved wins, tie broken by time penalty.
  • Languages: C, C++, Java, Python 3.

Scoring and Loot

  • Top 60 from Level 1 advance to Finals.
  • Gold, Silver, Bronze plus cloud credits, internship path, and regional seeding.

Fair Play

No plagiarism, no outside help, no generative AI during live rounds. Breaking rules means game over. Be honest, be sharp, have fun.
